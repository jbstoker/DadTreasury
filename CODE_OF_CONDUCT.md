# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Dad's Treasury a respectful, inclusive, and constructive experience for everyone.

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and identities.

---

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:
- being respectful and kind
- giving and accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the project
- showing empathy toward other community members
- being patient with newer contributors
- respecting accessibility and privacy concerns

---

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- harassment
- insulting or demeaning language
- personal attacks
- trolling
- privacy violations
- discriminatory behavior
- intimidation
- publishing private information without consent

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- issues
- pull requests
- discussions
- comments
- project chat spaces
- community events related to the project

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel or project contact address.

---

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from common open-source community standards and tailored for this project's privacy-first goals.
