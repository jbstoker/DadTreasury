# Chat

Dads Treasury includes secure, private parent-child messaging — fully offline.

## Opening Chat

- Open **Chat** from the Home screens quick nav grid, or:
- Parent: short tap on **Chat** in the quick nav
- Child: same

## Sending a Message

1. Type at the bottom text field
2. Tap **✉️ Send**

## Message Bubbles

- **Your messages** appear on the right (cyan tint)
- **The other person's messages** appear on the left (card color)
- Each bubble shows the time it was sent

## Privacy & Security

- Messages stay **on-device** — no cloud, no server
- No internet is required
- End-to-end encryption is planned for future sync adapters
- The message store is inside the encrypted SQLCipher database

## Why this design?

- Short messages are perfect for "I'm home! 🏠" or "Finished my chores!"
- The calm flat design reduces distraction
- No read receipts to cause anxiety
- Works even on older Android phones
