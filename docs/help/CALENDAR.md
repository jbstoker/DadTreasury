# Calendar & Nature Date

RetroNest shows **two calendars at once**: the familiar Gregorian calendar and the unique Natural 13-Month calendar.

## Gregorian Calendar

- The normal calendar you're used to
- Used for all events, reminders, and storage
- Example: **Tue, 8 Aug 2026 14:30**

## Natural 13-Month Calendar

A calm, nature-inspired calendar with:
- **13 months** of **28 days** each = **364 days**
- **1 Year Day** outside the months (day after December)
- **1 Leap Day** in leap years
- Always shown in **green** below the Gregorian date
- Example: **🌿 Leaf 11, Year 8**

The natural date is automatically derived from the real date — no need to calculate anything.

## Adding an Event

1. Open **Calendar** tab
2. Tap **+** (top right)
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** (required)
   - **Description**
   - **Date** (dd-MM-yyyy)
   - **Time** (HH:mm)
   - **Duration** in hours
   - **Reminder** minutes before (optional)
4. Tap **Create**

## Reminders

When you set a reminder, RetroNest schedules a local notification using the system alarm. It works offline and without internet.

- Works when the app is closed
- Uses AlarmManager (no cloud needed)
- Can be dismissed from the notification

## Viewing Events

The calendar shows upcoming events sorted by time. Each event displays:
- Title
- Gregorian time
- 🌿 Natural calendar time
