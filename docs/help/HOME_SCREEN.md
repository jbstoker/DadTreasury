# Home Screen

The Home screen is your dashboard. It adapts to your role.

## Parent View

- 📋 **Dashboard title**
- **Summary cards**:
  - Open Tasks — how many tasks are waiting to be done
  - To Approve — how many tasks need your approval
- **Quick navigation grid** with shortcuts to:
  - Tasks, Wallet, Calendar, Chat, Library, Locations, Pairing, Diagnostics

## Child View

- ⭐ **Today**
- **Summary cards**:
  - Open Tasks — tasks you can do
  - Completed — approved tasks
- **Quick navigation grid**:
  - My Tasks, My Wallet, Calendar, Chat, Library

## Today's Tasks

The bottom section shows tasks due today. Each row:
- Colored dot: green = approved, amber = awaiting, cyan = open
- Task title
- Time it's due

Tap a task to open its detail page.

## Why a Dashboard?

The Home screen makes the **next action clear** and keeps everything in one calm, predictable place — no clutter, no surprises.
