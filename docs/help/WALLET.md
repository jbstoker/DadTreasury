# Wallet & Time Bank

RetroNest keeps a secure ledger of all rewards — no unsafe single-number balance.

## Two Balances

| 💰 Wallet | ⏰ Time Bank |
|-----------|-------------|
| Money earned from Paid tasks (€) | Minutes earned from Time tasks |

## What's a Ledger?

Instead of storing just "€5.00", RetroNest stores **every transaction**:
- ✓ credit (reward)
- ✗ debit (spent)
- 💵 payout
- 🔧 correction
- ↩️ reversal

Your balance is **computed** from all transactions. This means:
- Every change is traceable
- Nothing can be silently altered
- Reversals cancel out mistakes honestly

## How to Earn

- Complete a task with **Paid** reward → Wallet credit
- Complete a task with **Time** reward → Time Bank credit
- Parent approves the task → reward is created immediately

## How to Spend (Parent)

1. Open **Wallet & Time Bank**
2. Tap **+** (top right)
3. Choose:
   - Wallet transaction: Credit / Debit / Correction
   - Time transaction: Credit / Debit / Correction
4. Enter amount (positive number)
5. **Save**

Use **Debit** when the child spends their money/time.

## Reading the Transaction List

Each row shows:
- 📝 note describing the transaction
- 📅 date and time
- 💵 amount with +/- coloring
  - Green = credit
  - Red = debit/payout
