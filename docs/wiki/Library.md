# 📖 Library (Family Wiki)

> The Library is a private, offline knowledge base for your family — a wiki that lives entirely on your device.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📂 Categories
- 📄 Pages
- 🔄 Revisions (history)
- 🏷️ Tags
- 🔍 Simple + detailed views

---

## 👨 Parent: Creating Categories

1. Open **Library**
2. Tap **📁 New Folder** icon (top right)
3. Enter a category name
4. Tap **Create**

## 👨 Parent: Creating Pages

1. Open **Library**
2. Tap **+** (top right)
3. Choose a category
4. Fill in **Title**, **Content**, and **Tags**
5. Tap **Create**

> 🔒 **Children can read** pages but **cannot** add or edit them.

---

## 🔍 Reading Pages (Both Roles)

1. Tap a category chip to filter
2. Tap a page to open it in a dialog
3. Content, tags, and revision number are shown

---

## 🔗 Sharing the Wiki Between Parent Apps

With **Connect Parent Apps** enabled, you can share wiki pages with another parent app:

1. Open **Connect** from the home grid
2. Tap a connected parent
3. Tap **Share Wiki**
4. Pick a page to share

See [Connect Parent Apps](Connect-Parent-Apps) for details.
