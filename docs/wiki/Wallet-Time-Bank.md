# 💰 Wallet & Time Bank

> Dad's Treasury keeps a **secure ledger** of all rewards — no unsafe single-number balance.

---

## 🎯 Two Balances

| 💰 **Wallet** | ⏰ **Time Bank** |
|---------------|------------------|
| Money earned from Paid tasks (€) | Minutes earned from Time tasks |

---

## 📒 What's a Ledger?

Instead of storing just "€5.00", Dad's Treasury stores **every transaction**:

- ✅ **Credit** (reward)
- ❌ **Debit** (spent)
- 💵 **Payout**
- 🔧 **Correction**
- ↩️ **Reversal**

Your balance is **computed** from all transactions. This means:

- Every change is **traceable**
- Nothing can be **silently altered**
- Reversals cancel out mistakes honestly

---

## 💪 How to Earn

1. Complete a task with **Paid** reward → Wallet credit
2. Complete a task with **Time** reward → Time Bank credit
3. Parent approves the task → reward is created immediately

> ⏰ Time rewards also trigger a **Google Family Link** screen-time grant. See [Google Family Link](Google-Family-Link).

---

## 🛠️ How to Spend (Parent Only)

1. Open **Wallet & Time Bank**
2. Tap **+** (top right) — only visible for the **parent** role
3. Choose:
   - Wallet transaction: Credit / Debit / Correction
   - Time transaction: Credit / Debit / Correction
4. Enter amount (positive number)
5. **Save**

Use **Debit** when the child spends their money/time.

> 🔒 **Children cannot add or remove funds.** The + button is hidden and the data layer rejects child attempts.

---

## 📃 Reading the Transaction List

Each row shows:

- 📝 note describing the transaction
- 📅 date and time
- 💵 amount with +/- coloring
  - 🟢 Green = credit
  - 🔴 Red = debit / payout
