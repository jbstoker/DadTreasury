# 🌈 My Boundaries & Choices

A **step-by-step guidance wizard** for neurodivergent children. One question at a time, low cognitive load, prevents decision paralysis and masking.

## How it works

The wizard shows **one question at a time** with a progress bar and a **Reset** button.

| Step | Question | Options | Branching |
|------|----------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | Energy Check | 🟢 Full / 🟡 Low / 🔴 Empty | 🔴 Empty → jumps to End screen |
| 2 | Motivation | 💚 I want to / 🤝 For friends / 😰 Pressured/Forced | 😰 Pressured/Forced → jumps to End screen |
| 3 | Role Comfort | 🎮 Active Player / 👀 Quiet Helper or Watcher | Continues |
| 4 | Practical | Cleanup: split / someone else · Time: 15 min / see how it goes | "Finish & See My Plan" |

## Early exits

If the child picks **Empty** or **Pressured/Forced**, all remaining questions are skipped and a validating message appears:

> *"Your battery is out. You have full permission to walk away and recharge. Let's find your safe space."*

> *"You never have to force yourself to fit in at the cost of your comfort. Your true friends will understand."*

## End screen

Two texts are generated from the answers:

1. **Public Text** (for friends/adults) — e.g. *"I'm going to sit this one out and watch/help from here!"* — with **Copy & Show**
2. **Home Team Action** (for Dad) — e.g. *"Dad, my battery is empty. Can you help me make an excuse to leave politely?"* — with **Copy** and **Send to Dad** (appears in your chat)

## Where to find it

Child home screen → **My Boundaries** tile (❤️)
